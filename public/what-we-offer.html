Your stay

Your perfect stay awaits

Whether you're looking for a cozy studio or a spacious family room, we have the ideal accommodation for your vacation in Metajna Pag. Explore our diverse options designed for your comfort and convenience.

Accommodation

Our accommodation types

We offer a variety of apartments and rooms to suit every need. Our one-bedroom, two-bedroom, and studio apartments, along with family rooms (quad, triple, and double), provide flexible options for individuals, couples, and families.

Amenities

Comfort and amenities

Every room, apartment, and studio comes equipped with air conditioning, Wi-Fi, and a smart TV with Netflix. All units include a refrigerator, kettle, glasses, cups, and private parking. While some have private balconies and full kitchens, others offer a charming table on a shared terrace with sea views. Rooms without kitchens are often rented with breakfast and/or dinner options.
