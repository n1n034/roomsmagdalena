Local tips & guides

Explore the best of Metajna Pag

Discover hidden gems, local favorites, and unforgettable experiences with our curated tips and guides for your stay in Metajna Pag.

Local spots

Our favorite local spots

We love sharing our favorite spots with guests to help you experience the true beauty of Metajna Pag. Don't miss these incredible locations:

  • Beaches: Plaža Metajna, Plaža Ručica, Plaža Zaglava, Plaža Beritnica
  • Restaurants: Konoba Sidro, Konoba Težak, Konoba Lončarić, Bistro Metajna, Restoran Riva
  • Hidden Gems: Life on Mars trail, Paški trokut, Sirana Gligora, Muzej grada Novalje, Antički vodovod Novalja
Activities

Popular activities and experiences

Make the most of your visit with these popular activities and experiences nearby:

  • Hiking: Explore the stunning "Life on Mars trail" for breathtaking views, and reach the peak of Vrh Sv. Vid.
  • Local Culture: Visit Sirana Gligora for delicious local cheese, and experience the vibrant "Fešta od sira Kolan."
  • Boat Trips: Embark on an unforgettable boat trip to the beautiful islands of Silba and Olib.
Good to know

Tips for an even better stay

To enhance your experience in Metajna Pag, here are a few special tips and advice from Rooms Magdalena:

  • Best time to visit: For fewer crowds and pleasant weather, consider visiting during the shoulder seasons (May-June or September-October).
  • Local customs: Embrace the relaxed pace of island life. A friendly "Dobar dan" (Good day) goes a long way!
  • Explore on foot: Many of our recommended spots are best explored by walking, allowing you to discover hidden corners.
  • Taste local specialties: Don't leave without trying the famous Pag cheese and fresh seafood at a local konoba.
