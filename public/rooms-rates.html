Discover the ideal room or apartment at Rooms Magdalena for your unforgettable vacation in Metajna Pag, Croatia. From cozy double rooms to spacious family studios, we have something for everyone. Find your perfect home away from home.

Accommodation

Our rooms & apartments

Rooms Magdalena offers a variety of accommodations designed to suit your needs, whether you're traveling solo, as a couple, or with family. Each unit is thoughtfully equipped to ensure a comfortable and enjoyable stay.

Pricing & booking

Double rooms

We offer one comfortable double room, perfect for couples or solo travelers seeking a peaceful retreat. Enjoy your private bathroom, refrigerator, unlimited Wi-Fi, and a Smart TV with complimentary Netflix. A kettle, glasses, mugs, and spoons are provided, along with welcome Nescafé and various teas. Two of our rooms feature a balcony with stunning views of our unique Pag stone, and all three rooms have a table on the shared terrace overlooking the sea.

Photos & details

Triple rooms

Our two triple rooms are ideal for small families or friends. Each comes with a private bathroom, refrigerator, unlimited Wi-Fi, and a Smart TV with complimentary Netflix. You'll also find a kettle, glasses, mugs, and spoons, along with welcome Nescafé and various teas. Two of these rooms offer a balcony directly from the room with views of our Pag stone, and all three have a table on the shared terrace overlooking the sea.

Photos & details

Family studios (2+2)

We feature two family studios designed for up to four guests (2+2). These studios include a equipped kitchen, allowing you to prepare your own meals. Dine on terrace with a breathtaking sea view, perhaps with a glass of local wine. Each studio has a double bed and bunk beds for children, along with unlimited Wi-Fi, air conditioning, and a Smart TV with complimentary Netflix.

Photos & details

Small studio (2 or 2+1)

Our cozy small studio is perfect for two or two plus one guest. It comes with a kitchen and bathroom, fully equipped with everything you need for meal preparation. Enjoy a table on the shared terrace with stunning sea views, unlimited Wi-Fi, air conditioning, and a Smart TV with complimentary Netflix.

Photos & details

Spacious two-bedroom & one-bedroom apartments

We also offer beautiful, spacious two-bedroom and one-bedroom apartments. These units include all the amenities listed above, ensuring a luxurious and comfortable stay for larger families or groups.
